# Implementation track

## Who reads, and what they are given

The first screen is the map and one choice. The choice is kept in this browser. It is not an account. Hindura clears it. After the choice, the screen keeps the tools that job uses and leaves the others out.

### Mini farm

A smallholder with one plot. The help is a season's advice for that place, and a yield to aim at that is not the large-farm harvest.

How: choose Mini farm. Tap the map, search, or say the village. Pick the crop. Read the cards. Save the aim. While a village name is being typed, the summary sheet steps aside so the matches stay visible. Choosing a match brings the sheet back.

The aim is the district harvest for all farms, Table 13. For Nyagatare maize that is 2,887 kg/ha. The survey does not print a mini-farm yield for one district, and the national smallholder rate is not copied onto the district.

Kept: the map, search, voice, the phone's location, the district-sector-cell-village ladder, the crop buttons, the advice, and the saved aim.

Left out: the crop catalog, the district map, and the method page. Those are for officers.

### Large farm

The same field, with a different aim. The aim is the large-farm harvest in Table 14 when that cell is printed. For Nyagatare maize that is 5,292 kg/ha. If that cell is blank, the aim falls back to the district harvest. A blank stays blank. It is not zero.

Kept and left out: the same as a mini farm.

### Sector agricultural officer

An extension officer for one sector. The survey prints no sector table, so the help is to name the sector and carry that district's advice. The system does not invent a sector yield.

How: choose Sector agricultural officer. Find the sector on the map, by search, by voice, or with the ladder. Open the district sheet from the link on the place.

Kept: the map, search, voice, location, and the ladder, opened on purpose. Crop buttons stay, so a question about one crop still reads the district figure. The district sheet and the method page stay available once the sector is known.

Left out: a personal yield aim, and the list of saved aims. Those belong to the farmer. The national crop catalog and the 30-district board are not the first screen.

### District agricultural officer

The officer for one district, comparing it with the others. The help is the action the rules name, irrigation or improved seed, and the maize yield against the milestone.

How: choose District agricultural officer. The map opens. Click the district. Switch the layer to see the milestone, the eight-year record, or the rainfall. Open Crops when the question is a yield other than the maize milestone. Open Method when the cutoff is the question.

Kept: the district map, the district sheet, the crop pages, and the method.

Left out: the farmer map. An officer is not saving a village target.

### Ministry

A planner reading the country. The help is every crop's national yield, the maize milestone, and where the shared levers fall.

How: choose Ministry. Open a crop. The bar is that crop against the highest in its group. Open the district map for irrigation, seed, and fertilizer, because those tables count every crop together. The milestone is maize only.

Kept: the crop advisor, the district map, and the method.

Left out: the farmer map.

Opening the site again uses the stored choice. A page opened with no choice still shows the full navigation, so a direct link is not blocked.

## Language

A control at the top reads en, fra, kin. It is kept in this browser, with the reader. en is English, fra is French, kin is Kinyarwanda. The choice screen, the field sheet, the advice cards, and the planner chrome follow it. The cited sentences on a district sheet and on the method page stay in English, because those are the checked readings.
