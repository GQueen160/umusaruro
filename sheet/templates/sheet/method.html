Method

What the sheet is

For each district, the sheet shows Season A 2026 maize yield against the PSTA 5 milestones, then names one lever, irrigation or all-crop improved seed, from fixed rules. Inorganic fertilizer is named beside the action when it also lags. The readers are a district agronomist and a planner at the Ministry of Agriculture and Animal Resources. They read the sheet; they do not type figures into it.

Season A is the main maize season, and its report is the latest that prints district maize yield and district input use split by farm type, smallholders against large farms. It is not the whole year.

The rules

The cutoffs are the team's, written into the evidence contract before the rainfall file was computed. They are not NISR thresholds.

  1. A district is an irrigation candidate when both irrigation cells (Table 31) are printed, the large-farm minus smallholder gap reaches the irrigation cutoff, and smallholder irrigation is under the line.
  2. A candidate gets irrigation as its action only when its September to February rainfall is below the median of the districts.
  3. Seed passes when the all-crop improved-seed gap (Table 20) reaches its cutoff.
  4. If both pass, the wider gap wins, unless seed leads irrigation by less than the lead cutoff; then irrigation stays.
  5. Fertilizer (Table 25) is named beside the action when its gap reaches its cutoff. It never replaces the action.
  6. A blank cell cannot open a gap. A district with neither lever passing gets no action.

Districts close to a cutoff

Within two points of a cutoff, one tenth of a point in a printed cell can change a label. These are listed so a reader can see how firm each label is.

Display rules

  • Yields are stored in kg/ha as printed. Tonnes per hectare means dividing by one thousand and rounding half up to two decimals. Above or below a milestone is decided on the kg/ha figure.
  • A dash, an empty column, or a district with no row is shown as blank, with the reason. It is never shown as zero.
  • Each statistic links to its source document. Hover the citation for the survey, table and year.

Why seed means all crops

Table 20 counts farmers who used improved seed on any crop. The maize-only figure is Table 21: {% stat maize_improved %} of maize seed was improved. That table is national only, with no district column and no farm-size column, so the sheet never shows it as a district figure and never calls Table 20 "maize seed".

Post-harvest loss is not a lever here: Table 16 puts maize post-harvest loss at {% stat maize_loss %} of use, in one national row.

Rainfall gate

Monthly CHIRPS v2.0 rainfall for September 2025 to February 2026 is summed per grid cell. A district's total is the mean of those sums at the grid cells whose centres fall inside the district boundary (geoBoundaries, Open Data Rwanda). The median of the district totals is {% stat median %}.

Rainfall gates irrigation only. It is not the farmer's reason for irrigating or not.

What the sheet does not claim

  • No maize variety: the survey records improved or traditional seed, not a variety name.
  • No yield gain from closing a gap. Each gap compares two kinds of farm.
  • No national rate copied onto a district.
  • No Season B or Season C figure in place of a Season A cell.
