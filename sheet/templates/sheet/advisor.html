An advisor for the whole cultivation journey

Umusaruro reads the published survey and keeps each finding under the crop it belongs to. The journey is the same for a farmer, a sector office, a district, and the ministry: before planting, in the field, and after harvest.

Season A 2026 Table 13 prints a yield for {% stat crop_count %}. Open a crop to see its district yields, and, where the report prints them, its national seed and harvest-loss rows. A blank cell stays blank.

Who is reading

Farmer

One field

Start on the farmer map. Walk district, sector, cell, and village, then choose the crop. The pin is the village. The yield is the district’s, because the survey is not published per village.

Sector

The district row

The survey does not print a sector table. A sector office reads the district it sits in. The place hierarchy still names the sector, so the field is not mixed up with another district.

District

Yield, then the shared levers

Each crop page lists that crop’s yield in all 30 districts. Irrigation, improved seed, and inorganic fertilizer are not in those rows. They cover every crop together on the district map.

Ministry

The national row

The national yield is the Rwanda row of the same table. The PSTA 5 yield milestone in this project is for maize. Other crops are read from the survey, not against that milestone.

The journey

Before

Before planting

Improved seed, where Table 21 names the crop. That table is national. It is not copied onto a district. The report has no soil test and no seed variety name.

Field

In the field

District yield from Table 13, and large-farm yield from Table 14. Maize also has the PSTA 5 milestones and the 2019 to 2026 yield series, on each district sheet.

After

After harvest

National use and post-harvest loss from Table 16, where that crop is printed. There is no district store in this survey.

Findings that belong to every crop

Tables 20, 25, and 31 do not name a crop. They count farmers using improved seed, inorganic fertilizer, and irrigation, for all crops together. Those findings, the rainfall gate, and the rules live on the district map and the method page. Each crop page points back there. They are not restated as if they were a maize-only result.
